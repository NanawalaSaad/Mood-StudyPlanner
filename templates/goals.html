⚠️ 50 SAVAGE RULES OF STUDY

Read this before you start. Break a rule, break your future.

01. Phone on DND. No exceptions.

02. Instagram is an illusion. Your career is reality.

03. If you open WhatsApp during study, subtract 1 hour.

04. Deep work means zero tabs open except study material.

05. Don't just read code. Write it. Break it. Fix it.

06. Computer Engineering is ruthless; stay updated or perish.

07. GTU papers require deep analysis, not just night-before reading.

08. 1 hour of focused coding > 5 hours of distracted reading.

09. Data Science isn't magic; it's math. Stop running from statistics.

10. Music without lyrics only. No Bollywood/Pop while studying.

11. Keep your desk clean. A messy desk is a messy mind.

12. Hydrate every 45 minutes.

13. Do not lie to the Planner. Honest hours only.

14. Attendance is non-negotiable. Show up.

15. Algorithms require pen and paper. Not just a screen.

16. If you are stuck on a bug for 1 hour, take a 10-minute walk.

17. No YouTube Shorts. They destroy attention span.

18. Sleep 7 hours. A tired brain writes bad code.

19. Master Python fundamentals before jumping to Machine Learning.

20. Learn to read Documentation. StackOverflow won't always save you.

21. Respect your posture. Sit straight.

22. A 5-minute break means 5 minutes. Not 30.

23. Don't memorize syntax. Understand the logic.

24. Commit to Git daily. Build your GitHub graph.

25. Your laptop is a weapon, not an entertainment box.

26. Stop complaining about syllabus. Be a self-learner.

27. If you skip a subject because it's hard, it will fail you.

28. Eat light before studying. Heavy food equals sleep.

29. Treat your projects like real-world startup products.

30. Consistency beats intensity. Code a little every day.

31. "I'll do it tomorrow" is the slogan of failures.

32. Track every hour. Time is your only asset.

33. Be savage about your goals. Don't compromise.

34. Learn one new tech concept every single day.

35. Stop multi-tasking. Brains don't process two things at once.

36. Read the error message properly before panicking.

37. Networking is important, but skills speak louder.

38. Do not study in bed. Use a chair and table.

39. Set daily targets before you sleep.

40. Avoid cheap dopamine. Earn your entertainment.

41. Revise. Your brain forgets 70% of data in 24 hours.

42. Build logic, not just beautiful UIs.

43. Teach what you learn to someone else.

44. Don't compete with classmates; compete with your yesterday.

45. Accept criticism on your code. Ego kills growth.

46. Learn to use Linux command line.

47. If it works, figure out WHY it works.

48. Take care of your eyes. Use blue light filters.

49. Trust the process. Mastery takes years.

50. Success is 100% your responsibility. No excuses.
